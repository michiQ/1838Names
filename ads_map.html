Business ads on the street grid

101 newspaper ads placed at 78 addresses. Tap an icon to read the ad. Drag to move the map; pinch, scroll or use the buttons to zoom. A dark circle with a number is several addresses close together: tap it to zoom in.

How the icons are placed. This is a drawn street grid, not a surveyed map: every block is the same size, and an icon is right to the block and the side of the street, not to the doorway. Philadelphia renumbered its houses in 1857, so ads from 1857 on are placed by house number (913 Lombard is in the 900 block, north side). Earlier ads carry old house numbers and are placed only from a cross street named in the ad. A dashed ring means the spot is approximate. Shippen Street is today's Bainbridge Street.
