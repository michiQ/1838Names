Philadelphia, 1838

Register of Trades of the Colored People

This register, printed in Philadelphia in 1838 by Merrihew and Gunn, lists the Black tradespeople of the city and its districts by trade, with their addresses. It marked with a star those who carried on business for themselves; of the others, it said, "about one-half work as journeymen, and the rest from choice or necessity follow other occupations."
