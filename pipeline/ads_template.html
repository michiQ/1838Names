Business Ads

Advertisements placed by Black business owners, teachers and institutions, cut from the pages of Freedom’s Journal (1827–1828) and the Christian Recorder (1854–1862). Undertakers, dentists, coal dealers, bakers, clothiers, boarding houses, music teachers. Click an ad to read it larger; click a name to open that person in the database.

Work in progress. More ads are added as we read more issues. Freedom’s Journal was published in New York, so some of its advertisers are New Yorkers.

Images are cropped from the newspaper page scans in the database: Freedom’s Journal courtesy the Center for Research Libraries, the Christian Recorder courtesy the Internet Archive. Each card links to its full page. © 1838 Black Metropolis, Inc., CC BY-NC-ND 4.0.
