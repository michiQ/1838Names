Where the businesses were

Business ads on the street grid

{{N_ON}} newspaper ads placed at {{N_PLACES}} addresses. Tap an icon to read the ad. Drag to move the map; pinch, scroll or use the buttons to zoom. Where businesses stood close together, their icons are lined up along the street in house-number order; zoom in to see them settle onto their blocks.

How the icons are placed. This is a drawn street grid, not a surveyed map: every block is the same size, and an icon is right to the block and the side of the street, not to the doorway. Philadelphia renumbered its houses in 1857, so ads from 1857 on are placed by house number (913 Lombard is in the 900 block, north side). Earlier ads carry old house numbers and are placed only from a cross street named in the ad. An icon with a dashed ring is an approximate spot: the ad gives an old house number or a small alley, so it is placed from the nearest cross street it names. Shippen Street is today's Bainbridge Street.
